Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This template is provided for convenience and is not legal advice. Review and adapt it to your organisation and jurisdiction before relying on it.

MICO360 GMap Scrapper (the “App”) is a tool that runs entirely on your own computer. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. We do not collect your data

The App runs locally on your machine. It has no backend servers that receive your searches or results. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or telemetry, and we do not receive, store, or sell any information about you or your activity.

2. Information the App processes on your device

  • The search queries and settings you enter.
  • Publicly available business information retrieved from Google Maps and from business websites — such as business names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings, opening hours, and email addresses that those websites publish.
  • Results are held in your browser and saved only to files you choose to export (XLSX/CSV) on your own computer.

3. Third-party services the App contacts

Requests originate from your computer to: Google Maps (to run searches), business websites (to read published contact details), and GitHub (to check for and download App updates). Those services have their own privacy policies, which govern their handling of those requests. No personal data of yours is transmitted during update checks.

4. Your responsibilities

Business contact data — including email addresses and phone numbers — may be “personal data” under laws such as the GDPR/UK GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL, and local data protection and anti-spam regulations. You are solely responsible for how you collect, store, and use data obtained with the App, including obtaining any required consent and honouring opt-out and deletion requests.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the current version.
